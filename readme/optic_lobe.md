## Datasets

This site serves these optic-lobe datasets:

- **Optic lobe, multiome annotation: Ozel et al. (2021)**: the scRNA-seq atlas from
  [Ozel et al. (2021)](https://www.nature.com/articles/s41586-020-2879-3),
  reclustered with updated labels.
- **Optic lobe, multiome annotation: Kurmangaliyev et al. (2020)**: the scRNA-seq atlas from
  [Kurmangaliyev et al. (2020)](https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0896627320307741),
  reclustered with updated labels.
- **Optic lobe: Ozel et al. (2021), annotated by Simon et al. (2026)**: the
  original clustering of the published objects at every stage, with the cluster
  annotation of [Simon et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1038/s41593-026-02256-6)
  (Supplementary Table, 41593_2026_2256_MOESM10_ESM.xlsx). Cells discarded in
  Ozel et al. (2021) are left out; the stage tSNEs are in the UMAP tab.

The _multiome annotation_ datasets were re-annotated in
[Treese et al. (2026)](https://doi.org/10.1073/pnas.2614314123) with a neural
network classifier trained on labels from the study's multiome developmental atlas.
