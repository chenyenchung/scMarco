## Datasets

This development site lists every converted dataset: the three optic-lobe
datasets (Ozel et al. 2021; Kurmangaliyev et al. 2020; reclustered in
Treese et al. 2026) and the two _dsx_-neuron datasets (four species; male and
female _D. melanogaster_). Tabs appear only when the selected dataset has the
data they need.
