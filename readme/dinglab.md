## Datasets

This site serves two datasets of _dsx_-expressing neurons:

- **Four species**: males of four _Drosophila_ species (_D. melanogaster_,
  _D. yakuba_, _D. santomea_ and _D. teissieri_).
- **Male and female melanogaster**: _D. melanogaster_ males and females.

Both include cell-level data, so the **UMAP** tab and the "% cells having
>0 UMIs" overlays are available.
